Employee Attrition Prediction – PeopleFirst Global Services

Capstone project to understand why employees leave, predict who is at risk of leaving, and suggest retention initiatives that HR can act on early.

Business Problem

PeopleFirst Global Services is a fast-growing multinational company in technology consulting, business process services and engineering. Many employees are leaving, which increases hiring and training costs and delays projects. Today, HR only reacts after someone resigns. This project aims to make retention proactive.

Business Objectives
Identify the reasons for employee attrition.
Identify employees who are planning to leave before they resign, so HR can take action early.
Find out which retention initiatives will help reduce attrition.
Analytical Objectives
Find out which factors are most related to employees leaving.
Build a machine learning model to predict which employees are at risk of leaving.
Identify the factors that made people stay longer, to help decide on retention initiatives.

Dataset
Historical HR data collected from several systems (HR, performance, engagement surveys, training and payroll).
Target: attrition_flag (1 = left, 0 = stayed)
Features: 35 columns covering demographics, job and career, compensation, performance, work patterns, training and engagement scores
Some legacy/reference columns (e.g. legacy_hr_record, payroll_reference_number) are expected to be removed during preprocessing

The dataset is provided for academic use and is not included in this repository. Place the file in data/raw/ before running the notebooks.
